\doxysection{Ticker}
\hypertarget{ticker}{}\label{ticker}\index{Ticker@{Ticker}}
This instruction toggles a GPIO pin repeatedly at a specified rate.~\newline


{\itshape \doxyparagraph*{Syntax\+: }}

{\itshape }

{\bfseries{ ticker GPIO\+\_\+\+NAME , Time\+Interval in\+Usec, Start/\+STOP =1/0,}}~\newline

\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item {\bfseries{GPIO\+\_\+\+NAME\+:}} Any digital GPIO
\item {\bfseries{Time\+Interval\+:}} Time required to activate the toggle function. It could be a variable or a number
\item {\bfseries{Start/\+Stop\+:}} A Boolean value. 1 starts the counter, and 0 stops the counter. \textbackslash{}tticker PA\+\_\+4 10000 1;\textbackslash{}t\textbackslash{}t~~~  rem PA\+\_\+4 will toggle every 10 milliseconds ~\newline
 ticker PA\+\_\+4, 1000, 1; ~~~  rem PA\+\_\+4 will toggle every 10 milliseconds ~\newline
 OR\+:~\newline

\end{DoxyItemize}
